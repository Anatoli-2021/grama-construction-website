The requested visual direction is minimal and precise, inspired by technology-company product storytelling: neutral sans-serif type, clear scale, useful space, strong real photography and a deliberate residence/detail/neighborhood transition. Preserve the current founder/realtor portraits, completed-project context, partner artwork and C5-derived lot plan. ## Investor narrative

The proposed audience is investors/property owners who need a capable partner to coordinate residential development. The presentation explains execution responsibility, visible project economics and defined decisions, followed by completed-project proof and the larger subdivision vision. Terms are agreed for a specific project rather than invented as a universal investment product.

The private working copy contains selected source-labelled underwriting inputs and a screening model. Actual data stays outside Git. Its cost/return distinctions matter: expected sale value is a projection; a budget is not incurred cost; an offer is not a closed sale; project screening spread is not an investor distribution or annual yield. Financing, carrying costs, reserves, taxes, fees and agreed distribution terms must remain explicit.
